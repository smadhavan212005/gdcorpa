How it works

A single attention backbone is augmented with two relative-position bias systems — Cartesian and polar — fused by a gate computed per query token, per attention head, per layer, instead of the usual dual-backbone late-fusion approach.

1
Dequantize — undo JPEG color blur back to 3 discrete classes (background / normal die / defective die)
2
Estimate wafer center — closed-form centroid + radius of gyration from the die mask
3
Gated dual-coordinate attention — per-token blend of Cartesian and polar relative-position bias
4
Three prediction heads — defect pattern (primary), failure mechanism, expected yield loss
